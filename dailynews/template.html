How the two scores work

Trend, 0 to 100

How big the story is right now. 60% is how many different outlets carry it, 15% how recent the newest article is, 15% how many articles appeared in the last 6 hours, and 10% how high it sits in each outlet's feed. A story only one outlet carries cannot score above 28.

Reliability, 0 to 100

How far you can trust that the story is accurate. It is the average rating of the three best outlets reporting it, plus 3 points for each further outlet rated 70 or more that also reports it (up to 9). Weak outlets repeating a story do not lower it.

High, 80+ Good, 65 to 79 Mixed, 45 to 64 Low, under 45

Limits

Outlet ratings are editorial estimates of each outlet's track record, kept in sources.json. They rate the outlet, not the individual article, and a high score is not proof. Stories are grouped automatically by headline wording, so a group can occasionally mix two related events.
