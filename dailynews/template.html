How the two scores work

Trend, 0 to 100

How big the story is right now. 50% is how many different outlets carry it, 18% is attention on social and search platforms (Reddit, Hacker News, Bluesky, Mastodon, Lemmy, Google Trends, Wikipedia page views), 12% how recent the newest article is, 12% how many articles appeared in the last 6 hours, and 8% how high it sits in each outlet's feed.

Reliability, 0 to 100

How far you can trust that the story is accurate. It is the average rating of the three best outlets reporting it, plus 3 points for each further outlet rated 70 or more that reports it under its own headline (up to 9). Outlets that only rerun another outlet's headline, such as wire copy, are not counted as extra confirmation. Weak outlets repeating a story do not lower it, and social media attention never raises it.

Typical account

The sentences under each headline are quoted from the outlets' own summaries. They are the ones that share the most with what every other outlet covering the story wrote, so they show the common version of events. Nothing is machine-written. Open a story to see how left-leaning, center, right-leaning and state-controlled outlets each headline it.

Limits

Outlet ratings are editorial estimates of each outlet's track record, kept in sources.json. They rate the outlet, not the individual article, and a high score is not proof. Stories are grouped automatically by headline wording, so a group can occasionally mix two related events, and a social post can occasionally be attached to the wrong story. X (Twitter) is not included because it offers no free access to read posts.

    Popular elsewhere, no matching story found

    The biggest items on each platform that could not be matched to a story from a rated outlet. Most are discussion, opinion or niche links rather than news, and a few may be about a story above in different words. Treat any factual claim here as unverified.

    Where this comes from

    Platforms are used only to measure attention. Outlets are what the reliability score is built on: each has a rating, a political lean, and a count of its articles inside the time window.
